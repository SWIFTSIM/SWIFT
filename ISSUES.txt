Black holes alone in a sub-cell miss neighbours in sibling sub-cells
--------------------------------------------------------------------

When a self task is split (scheduler_splittask_hydro() in
src/scheduler_splittasks.c), a pair task is only created between two progeny
if both contain gas, stars (with feedback or the BH star loop) or sinks.
A progeny holding only black holes is therefore never paired with its
siblings. Its black holes then miss the gas (BH density/swallow loops) and the
stars (BH star loop) in the sibling sub-cells, giving too-large smoothing
lengths / star search radii and wrong densities. This is pre-existing in master
(it affects the black hole gas loops there) and was inherited by the BH star
loop on this branch. It has not been seen in the EAGLE_6 runs, where the
brute-force BH star density check would flag it. A fix would be to also
accept progeny with (with_black_holes && progeny->black_holes.count) in both
conditions of that loop.
