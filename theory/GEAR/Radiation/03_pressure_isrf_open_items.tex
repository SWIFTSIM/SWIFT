\section{Open items (this document)}
\label{sec:isrf-open}

The two channels this logbook covers carry the tags \textbf{T6} and
\textbf{T8}, continuing the numbering of the HII photoionization
logbook's open-items list (\code{05\_open\_items.tex} in
\code{radiation.pdf}, which holds T1--T5, T7, T9--T11); the tags are
defined here because the work itself lives here.

\begin{itemize}
  \item[$\square$] \textbf{T6} -- FUV/ISRF coupling: photoelectric
    heating and H$_2$ photodissociation driven by a variable local
    interstellar radiation field, via Grackle's existing hooks (nothing
    needs changing inside Grackle --
    Sections~\ref{sec:fuv-lw-grackle}--\ref{sec:fuv-two-bands}). The
    band decision and both candidate propagation designs are recorded
    in Section~\ref{sec:fuv-isrf}; as that section's own summary notes,
    T6 as a whole remains unscoped as an implementation. Blocked on
    per-star $L_{\rm FUV}$/$L_{\rm LW}$ band luminosities -- two more
    band integrals of the same stellar-atmosphere tables the HII
    logbook's item T7 (\texttt{pychem} tables) would generate. Last on
    the roadmap together with T8.

  \item[$\square$] \textbf{T8} -- Radiation-pressure validation. The
    channel (Section~\ref{sec:radiation-pressure}) is implemented but
    off by default (\code{radiation\_pressure\_efficiency: 0} in every
    validated example) and has never been validated; the two-tier
    validation plan is Section~\ref{sec:rp-validation-plan}. Two bugs
    were already found and fixed during this item's code review
    (Section~\ref{sec:rp-bugs-20260723}: inverted $\kappa_{\rm IR}$
    unit conversion, stale $L_{\rm bol}$ past star death), plus the
    cosmological $d\ln a$-as-proper-time impulse bug fixed on the HII
    logbook's side. \textbf{Known open defect, deferred until this
    item is picked up}: the \code{RadiationPressureMomentumCheck}
    debug example crashes under \code{--enable-debugging-checks}.
    Last on the roadmap together with T6.
\end{itemize}
